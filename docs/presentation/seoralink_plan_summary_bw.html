3. Vital Operating Rules: Rolling Upgrade, Exit & Status Governance & Status

Rule A: Non-Cumulative Rolling Auto-Upgrade (Doubling Value)

Rank rewards do not accumulate additively ($10 + $20 + $40 ≠ $70). Instead, holding your reward automatically rolls 100% of the active tier balance into the entry fund of the next higher rank. Active holding value doubles at every level:
$10 → $20 → $40 → $80 → $160 → $320 → $640 → $1,280 → $2,560 → $5,120 → $10,240 → $20,480.

Rule B: Intermediate Ranks 1 to 11 Withdrawal (20% Reserve • Permanent ID Ban)

A participant can initiate an exit withdrawal at any intermediate rank (Zen through Legend):
• 80% Net USDT: Credited immediately to external wallet (20% protocol deduction retained for global queue liquidity).
• Permanent Account Deactivation / Ban: Initiating a rank withdrawal between Rank 1 and Rank 11 permanently terminates the ID. The ID is permanently banned and CANNOT be reactivated in the future.
• Strict Forfeiture: Forfeits all direct sponsor commissions ($0.50), 5% team overrides, and queue upgrades forever.

Rule C: Pinnacle Ultima (Rank 12) Privilege (10% Fee • Lifetime Active ID)

Members holding through all 12 ranks achieve Apex Cycle Completion at Rank 12 (Ultima):
• 10% Protocol Fee (90% Net): Pays out $18,432.00 USDT Net on the $20,480.00 gross rank valuation.
• Rank Queue Concludes: The rank queue progression ends for this ID (no further rank pools).
• Lifetime Active Status (Exclusive): Unlike R1–R11, an Ultima ID remains permanently ACTIVE for life. The member can continue personally sponsoring new direct members ($0.50 direct bonus) AND continuously receives the 5% team mentorship overrides as team members upgrade ranks!
• Strict Exclusivity: This lifetime active earning privilege is valid ONLY for Ultima (Rank 12) IDs.

Rule D: Commission Withdrawal Policy (Flat 10% Deduction)

All affiliate commission withdrawals (Direct Sponsor 5% [$0.50] and Upline Mentorship 5% Overrides) carry a flat 10% protocol deduction across all ranks.
